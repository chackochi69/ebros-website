About Ebros

Transitioning from forensic precision capture to the forefront of national spatial data infrastructure.

Our Strategic Pivot & Evolution

Ebros was established to deliver precision forensic inspection technologies, assessing structural conditions, analyzing material risks, and auditing physical building properties with engineering accuracy.

Recognizing that physical spaces were completely decoupled from modern digital workflows, we leveraged our deep inspection and scanner expertise to build advanced visual spatial software. We expanded our capabilities from forensic analytics to absolute spatial intelligence—building **Edict Veritas**, the core technology platform designed to structure, stage, and simulate complete built environments with precision.

Our Mission

Transform complex physical environments into highly-actionable, beautifully styled, and forensic-grade digital structures that optimize commercial value.

Our Vision

To construct India's premier physical spatial operating engine, empowering infrastructure stakeholders with reliable real-time property intelligence.
